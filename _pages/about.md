Heath N. Landress, DHS, PhD, is an independent researcher and a retired U.S. Navy Chief Petty Officer with 23 years of service. He is a doctoral graduate of Walden University, where he earned both a Doctor of Human Services and a PhD in Human Services. His research examines the intersection of online extremism, youth exploitation, digital grooming, and platform safety. He is the originator of the Aesthetic-to-Risk Framework of Nihilistic Violence Aesthetics (ART-NVA) and the Youth Online Exploitation–Extremism Interface (YOEEI).

His work develops observable, evidence-based methods for distinguishing transgressive online performance from content that is moving toward warning-behavior concern. His applied research interests include child protection, prevention science, youth-facing media literacy, digital risk assessment, and platform trust-and-safety practice. Following 23 years of service in the U.S. Navy, Dr. Landress remains committed to research that strengthens protections for children and young people in online environments.
